# Idle Clash: the page map (IA)

Session 0 · October 2026. This file is the contract between the shell and the features. The shell owns pages and their layout; features fill named slots with data; shared components draw it. A feature never adds a page, and a new page or menu entry is the owner's decision. `pnpm verify` checks that every slot a feature fills is listed here.

## 1. The menu

Six entries, in this order (the sixth, Castle Clash, the owner's choice in session 28):

| Entry | Page | What it answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hearth | Home | What is happening, what happened while I was away, and what do I aim for next? |
| Skills | The seven skills and each skill's page | What can I do, and what does the next level give? |
| Combat | Gear, foes, food | What should I fight, with what, and what are my odds? |
| Storehouse | Items, the vendor, the collection log | What do I have, where does it come from, and what is it for? |
| Castle Clash | A castle of your own: its edict, troops, sieges and spies (opens at total level 30) | What does my castle give me, and whom can I challenge? |
| Hiscores | Ladders and who is online (on a phone, in the account menu rather than the menu at the foot: session 130) | How do I compare? |

Outside the menu:

- **A fault** (session 108): a page that throws while drawing is replaced by "Something broke here" with Reload, under the header and menu, and reported to the server's log; a fault in the shell itself says the same on the page's own ground.
- **The notices tray**: a bell in the header with a count of new notices and unread messages. Two tabs: Notices (lane stops, goals claimed, the away recap) and Messages (session 45).
- **The account menu** (session 130, the owner): the player's picture at the top right opens a small popup under it: Hiscores, Deeds, Your profile (their own card) and Settings (privacy, terms, sign out, and, session 9, delete account). A cross in its top right, Escape (which hands the focus back), a press outside, the focus tabbing out of it (session 132) or choosing a place closes it. On a phone, where Hiscores has no room at the foot, the picture is ringed in woad while the hiscores are open.
- **The tour**: a short overlay on a player's first visit, pointing at slots by name, never at layout classes. Its end, the tray's last reading and the offer of notices while away are kept for each player in a browser (session 204), not for the device.
- **A phone's edges (session 206):** the page is drawn edge to edge, and nothing read or pressed stands under a notch, rounded corners, status bar or home bar: each bar keeps its own spacing with the phone's reported inset added.
- **Closing what opens (session 42, the owner's rule):** every popup (a sheet, a moment of any size, the tour's card) closes from a cross in its top right; verify fails a popup without one.

## 2. The shell

Built at 320 px first. Three widths are specified and checked: 320, 390 and 1,440 px. Container queries decide each section's arrangement; the page never scrolls sideways. Every view also holds on a 280 px phone (a fold's cover screen, a small phone at its largest display size): no sideways scroll and no words past the screen's edge (session 157).

Between 768 and 1,023 px the rail collapses to icons (64 px) and the lanes panel becomes the phone's lanes strip under the header.

**The frame** (session 131, the owner: the desktop should look like a high-end game). From 768 px the header and the rail are drawn in the lodge's night in either theme (what is in them takes the dark theme's colours), the page you are on marked on the rail in gold leaf with a bar at its left. From 1,024 px the rail's head carries the crest (the game's mark and name, level with the header, whose own name then gives way) and its foot who is playing (their picture in its frame, name, title or rank, total level; not on a window under 620 px tall); panels, a skill's head and buttons stand a little off the page, lit along their top edge, each panel's title marked with gold leaf; the Storehouse's tiles are slots that lift under the pointer; each lane in the lanes panel is its own tile edged on the left by how it stands (moss at work, grey at rest, the alert's colour stopped). From 1,600 px the page column widens to 1,080 px. Phones keep their own look. A phone on its side (the rail, under 520 px tall) closes the rail's rows up to 52 px so every place stays on the screen, and the rail scrolls if even that is too tall (session 132).

**The realm's banner** (session 235): while the keepers of the realm have a banner up (put up from the console's Live section for an hour to a week), it stands at the head of the page column on every page of the game, edged in woad: "From the keepers of the realm" over its words (up to 200 characters), and a cross (Put the banner away) at the end of its first line. Put away, it stays away for that player on that browser until a new banner goes up; it goes from every open page the moment it is taken down or its time runs out. It is in no slot: most of the time it is not there.

Density rules from the brief apply everywhere: no full-width section holding one or two lines, no scroll when the content would fit, one control drawn one way.
