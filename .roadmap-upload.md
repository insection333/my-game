## Throughout: simple to use, every popup dismissable (owner, 3 November)

- **Every popup has a close control in its top right:** a cross, or a Close button. That covers sheets, moments (centre, banner and toast), the tour's cards, the notices tray, the profile card and the inbox.
- **Verify enforces it:** a popup without a close control in its top right fails the build.
- **Simplicity:** each new feature adds as few new screens and controls as it can, and reuses the game's own components.

## Phase 1: core gameplay and strategic depth (session 42)

- **Fun audit of the core loop.** Look at the pacing of combat and progression across the first hour, the first week and the late game, using the simulation's numbers and play-throughs. Find flat or repetitive stretches and fix them in the rules, not only in the presentation.
- **Feedback for the moments that matter.** Crisp transitions and micro-animations for:
  - critical hits and big hits in a fight;
  - victories (a foe slain, a boss, a battle won);
  - rank-ups (level, mastery, hiscores rank, title);
  - unlocks (a new foe, region, recipe or honour).
- **The arena.** The fight in the Combat lane is played out on the Combat page:
  - both sides' health draining, swing by swing;
  - hits and misses, and critical hits (the top tenth of rolls) in gold;
  - food eaten as it happens;
  - "Slain" when the foe falls.
- **Castle Clash battle reports (owner, 3 November).** A Reports tab of its own, easy to read and good to look at:
  - each siege, defence, stronghold, fortress and spy mission as a report card;
  - each card shows both sides' arms, the outcome in a word and colour, the clash bar, and what it moved, with icons;
  - filters: All, Sieges, Defences, Strongholds and fortresses, Spies;
  - each card opens the full account, redrawn as a battle report: a victory or defeat banner, both sides' strength and formation, the day's fortune, the spoils, and the walls;
  - spy reports show the mission's icon, success or caught, and what was learned;
  - the War tab keeps the latest few, with a link to the Reports tab.
- **Limits.** Motion stays quiet and short, follows reduced motion, and stays inside verify's CPU budget. Sound stays off until switched on.
- **Close controls.** Every popup gets its close control, and verify gains the check for it.

### Inbox (session 45)

- **Where it lives.** Inside the bell's tray, as a second tab, with the badge counting both.
- **Rules:**
  - plain text only (markup stripped, control characters removed), 250 characters at most;
  - a cooldown between sends;
  - a daily cap on how many different players you can message;
  - no messages to a player who has blocked you;
  - words caught by the profanity filter (stricter than the names filter, and able to see through spacing and symbols) are refused, and the sender is warned. Repeat attempts are flagged for the admin.
- **Block and Unblock (owner, 3 November):**
  - blocking stops only mail between the two players;
  - it never touches sieges, ladders, strongholds or anything else in the game;
  - Unblock sits beside every block, and on a list of blocked players in the inbox.
- **Storage:**
  - 20 messages per inbox, with the oldest replaced first;
  - read messages deleted after 7 days and unread ones after 14, in the housekeeping sweep;
  - all of a player's mail erased when they delete their account.
- **Admin mail monitor:**
  - search by player and by text, filter by flagged;
  - see the threads between two players, and the attempts the filter refused;
  - act from the monitor: warn, mute messaging, or ban.
- **Disclosure (owner, 3 November):** that moderators can read messages is stated in the terms of service only, and nowhere else (not in the inbox, not in the privacy policy).
