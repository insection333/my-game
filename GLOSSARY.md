# Idle Clash: glossary

Every player-facing term, with the one meaning it has everywhere. Generated by `pnpm verify` from `packages/content/src/glossary.ts`: add or change a term there. The content check fails when a string in `packages/content/src/strings/en.ts` uses a term to avoid.

## Voice

Plain, warm and brief, with an old-world flavour in names rather than in instructions. Say what happened and what to do next: "Smithing stopped: out of tin ore". Sentence case everywhere. Numbers always come from data through the formatter, never typed into text.

## Terms

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Action | One thing a lane repeats: chopping a pine, forging a helm, fighting a foe |
| Advantage | Your style beats the foe's: you deal more damage and take less |
| AP | Action points in Castle Clash: earned by the hour and by the Call to arms, spent on drilling, spies, sieges and incursions |
| Arena | The fight in your Combat lane played out on the Combat page, swing by swing: the very fight the game settles |
| Arms | Castle Clash: the shield each lord, stronghold and fortress bears, its own and the same every time |
| Artisan lane | The lane that turns materials into bars and gear (Smithing), fish into food (Cooking) and bars and logs into arrows (Fletching) |
| Away | Time you are not on the page; lanes keep working for up to 24 hours |
| Away recap | What happened while you were away, best thing first |
| Banner | The colour your plaque and your hiscores pennant fly, opened by deeds |
| Bar | Metal smelted from ore: copper, bronze, iron, steel |
| Boss | A region's last and hardest foe: met at an expedition's end, or fought alone once a day |
| Call to arms | Castle Clash: answered once a day for AP held in reserve; days in a row build a streak that adds to the hourly AP |
| Castle Clash | The castle each lord raises at total level 100: an edict for the rest of the game, troops, spies and sieges |
| Castle rating | A castle's lasting strength from its buildings, troops and best relics; lords are matched near it |
| Collect | Close the away recap: everything in it is already in your Storehouse |
| Collection log | The record of every find you have made, by skill |
| Combat lane | The lane that fights; trains your weapon's style and Hitpoints |
| Comes from | On an item: every action that produces it |
| Cooked food | Food Cooking makes from fish and logs; it heals more for its worth than the vendor's |
| Daily goal | One larger goal a day |
| Critical hit | A hit in the top tenth of what you can deal, marked in gold in the arena |
| Decree | Castle Clash: the realm's rule for the week, the same for every castle |
| Deed | A feat done in the course of play (a level, work, mastery, foes, finds, the fire), easy, hard, elite or master; listed on the Deeds tab of Hiscores |
| Deed points | What deeds give by tier (easy 5, hard 15, elite 40, master 100); they open titles, a banner and frames |
| Disadvantage | The foe's style beats yours: you deal less damage and take more |
| Dish | A pie or a stew: Cooking mixes a tier's fish with the one before, and it heals a quarter more |
| Drill | Castle Clash: AP spent on a troop for a lasting bonus, up to half again |
| Drop | An item a foe leaves behind |
| Easy, Fair, Costly, Too strong | Your odds verdicts, by how much food a fight costs and whether you would retreat |
| Edict | Castle Clash: a lift to XP, fight gold or finds across the game while the treasury holds what it needs |
| Enlarge | Buy more Storehouse slots with gold |
| Expedition | A run into a region for 1, 2, 4 or 8 hours in the Combat lane: fight stages, caches and camps, twists, a fork, and the region's boss at the end |
| Find | A rare extra from an action, at one of three rarities |
| First steps | The six opening goals, each with a button |
| Foe | Something to fight; each has a style, shown before you fight |
| Fork | Halfway along an expedition: two roads, one chosen before setting out, each changing the second half |
| Frame | The metal rim of your plaque on the Hearth, opened by deed points |
| Gathering lane | The lane that cuts wood, mines ore, fishes or draws runes; the fire opens a second one |
| Gold | Earned from selling and from foes; buys food and Storehouse space |
| Health | How much damage you can take; set by Hitpoints |
| Hearth | The home page |
| Hiscores | The ladders of every skill and the total |
| Hitpoints | The skill that raises health, opens foes and lets you wear armour; trained by every fight |
| Honours | Your title, banner and frame: chosen on the Deeds tab, seen by everyone |
| Incursion | Castle Clash: an assault on a fortress, tier after tier, for war marks and drill |
| Key | Opens a tier's standing stones for Runecraft; Smithing binds it from two bars, and it is kept |
| Kindling | Each long stretch of XP a skill earns past 99 (the skill page says how far): a flame on its seal and, for the first five, a stronger Path. Nothing is reset |
| Lane | One of your workers: Gathering, Artisan, Combat; each runs one action at a time |
| Lasting mark | The bonus a Legendary find gives the first time you find it |
| Legendary | The rarest find, about 1 in 5,000 actions |
| Level | A step in a skill, 1 to 40; every level unlocks something |
| Marks | What expeditions and bosses pay, spent on relics |
| Mastery | How practised you are at one action; each step makes it faster and richer |
| Medallion | A deed's mark on the Deeds tab, ringed in its tier's colour and filled once done |
| Mercenary stronghold | Castle Clash: one of five targets made for you each day near your rating, the harder paying more; it falls for the day once breached, and its bounty comes from the war chest |
| Milestone | A long goal, such as total level 100 |
| Next action | The action queued behind a lane's current one; it starts when the current one stops |
| Notices | The tray of things that happened and need you |
| Orders of the day | Castle Clash: three small aims each day, the day's strongholds always among them; each pays war marks when done, and all three pay more |
| Path | A level-99 skill's way of working: Bounty (more of what it makes), Lore (more XP) or Fortune (more finds); free to change |
| Perk | A lasting bonus unlocked at a level |
| Rank | Your standing, from Wanderer to Legend of the Realm, rising with your total level |
| Rare | A find of about 1 in 500 actions |
| Region | Where foes are fought: the march, the Sunken Fen and the Ashen Peaks, each a tab of Combat |
| Relic | A lasting boost bought with marks, in ranks: XP in a skill, food, finds or Storehouse space |
| Rout | Castle Clash: all five of a day's mercenary strongholds taken, worth war marks of its own |
| Retreat | Leaving a fight at low health with no food; nothing is lost and the lane stops |
| Short goals | Three rolling goals of 10 to 15 minutes |
| Siege | Castle Clash: an attack on another lord's castle for the treasury gold their vault cannot hide |
| Slot | A space in the Storehouse for one kind of item, in any amount |
| Stage | Ten minutes of an expedition's fighting against one foe; six an hour |
| Steading | The works you raise around your Hearth with gold and your own materials, each a lasting gain shown in the Hearth's scene |
| Stoke | Keep the fire burning |
| Storehouse | Where everything you own is kept |
| Storehouse full | No free slot for a new kind of item: a lane that would make one stops |
| Style | Melee, Archery or Magic: set by the weapon you wear |
| Supply | A style's extra damage, one used a foe: whetstones for Melee, arrows for Archery, runes for Magic |
| Supply cache | What short goals and the daily goal pay in: gold for certain, and a draw for more |
| The fire | Burns while you play: more XP and a second Gathering lane |
| Tier | A rank of material and gear: Copper, Bronze, Iron, Steel |
| Title | A name shown beside yours on the hiscores and on your plaque, opened by deed points or a particular deed |
| Total level | Every skill's level added together |
| Tempering | Castle Clash: war marks and gold for a chance to strengthen a relic at its last level; the chance falls with each temper, never below 5%, and a masterstroke tempers twice |
| The hidden armoury | Castle Clash: now and then a fortress taken yields its war marks twice over; once in a hundred, a hoard, five times over |
| Treasury | Castle Clash: gold kept in your castle; it powers the edict, and part of it can be raided |
| Twist | Something that holds for a whole expedition, giving and taking: fog, hungry wolves, a storm |
| Uncommon | The most frequent find, about 1 in 50 actions |
| Unlock | What a level gives: an action, a recipe, a tier, a foe, a food or a perk |
| Used in | On an item: every recipe and use that takes it |
| Vendor | Buys anything you sell; sells food |
| War chest | Castle Clash: the realm's share of gold spent in castles, from which mercenary bounties are paid |
| War marks | Castle Clash: earned in battle and incursions; they pay for the War College and war relics |
| War relic | Castle Clash: one of nine relics forged with war marks, three worn at once |
| Work | One of the six buildings of the steading, raised five times, each time for a little more |
| XP | Experience; earned by actions, it raises levels |
| Your odds | The fight engine's verdict on a foe with your current gear and food |
